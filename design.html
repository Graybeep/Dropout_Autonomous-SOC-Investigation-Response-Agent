Why trust it

An agent that confidently gets it wrong is worse than no agent. Six things keep this one honest.

1

It shows its evidence

Every verdict names the facts behind it. Nothing is claimed without something to point at.

2

It cannot invent

If it claims something it has not actually checked, the claim is rejected and it has to go and check.

3

Confidence is calculated

The agent never picks its own confidence number. That is worked out from the evidence it found, the same way every time.

4

It checks its own actions

After blocking an attacker it goes back and confirms the block really happened, instead of assuming.

5

It admits when it cannot tell

If evidence is missing or something breaks mid-investigation, it says so and lowers its confidence rather than guessing.

6

A person always wins

An analyst can overrule any verdict. The agent obeys, records both views, and does not quietly undo the decision.

The one that matters most

It never assumes its own action worked

Asking for something to happen and it actually happening are two different things.

1. Decide
The evidence says this attack was real
2. Act
Block the attacker
3. Go back and look
Check whether the block is genuinely in place
4. Record the truth
If it did not take effect, the case says so
