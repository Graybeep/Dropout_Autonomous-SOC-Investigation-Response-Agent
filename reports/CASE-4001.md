**Hypothesis:** For this attack to have SUCCEEDED, three things must all be true:
1. SRV-FIN-07 runs a database/service version inside the affected range of a SQL-injection-relevant CVE (i.e., actually vulnerable).
2. The host-side logs show the injected query actually executed / returned data rather than erroring or being rejected.
3. The packet flow shows data leaving the host (exfil), not just a small failed request.

**Falsifiers:** a patched version outside all affected ranges; logs showing auth failure/syntax error/blocked; a tiny request/response flow with no outbound payload.
